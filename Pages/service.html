OUR SERVICES

One partner for every stage of your
packaging system.

01

Installation & Commissioning

Our certified field engineering team delivers complete turnkey on-site installation and commissioning services. We handle precision positioning, utility hookups, refrigeration line integration, electrical power verification, and multi-point calibration. Every installation includes full system run-in, safety interlock testing, and hands-on operational walkthroughs to ensure seamless facility integration from day one.

02

Fabrication & Customization

We provide precision structural and custom metal fabrication tailored to unique laboratory constraints and complex specimen requirements. Utilizing high-grade SS304/SS316 stainless steel, heavy-duty thermal insulation, custom airflow ducts, reinforced floor grids, and specialized specimen fixturing, our fabrication team ensures your chambers and packaging lines operate with absolute structural integrity and optimal thermal dynamics.

03

Repair & Overhaul

When unexpected equipment breakdowns occur, our rapid-response service engineers diagnose and restore operations with minimal downtime. We provide complete component-level troubleshooting, compressor rebuilds, refrigeration leak detection, sensor replacements, and electrical panel rewiring. All repairs utilize genuine OEM components and adhere to stringent factory performance specifications.

04

AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts)

Provert's structured Annual Maintenance Contracts provide comprehensive preventive care, scheduled quarterly health checkups, prioritized emergency breakdown support, and discounted spare parts. Our proactive maintenance routines optimize energy consumption, extend equipment service life, prevent costly mid-cycle failures, and ensure audit-ready compliance year-round.

05

Consultation & Engineering Audits

Our senior thermal and reliability engineers offer specialized technical consultation for facility planning, test protocol development, and energy optimization. Whether you are expanding a testing laboratory, migrating to eco-friendly refrigerants, or aligning testing procedures with global automotive (ISO 16750), aerospace (MIL-STD), or pharmaceutical (ICH Q1A) standards, we provide actionable engineering guidance.

06

Calibration & Validation

Ensure unwavering test measurement certainty with our comprehensive on-site calibration and thermal mapping services. Using NABL-traceable reference instrumentation, we conduct multi-point temperature, humidity, and airflow uniformity validations. Every service includes official calibration certificates, deviation reports, and traceable compliance documentation for ISO/IEC 17025 and regulatory audits.

07

Technical Support & Operator Training

Maximize operational productivity with our dedicated 24/7 technical assistance and structured staff training programs. We provide hands-on operator guidance for touchscreen PLC programming, cycle profile automation, daily maintenance routines, and remote telemetry diagnostics, ensuring your team has the expertise to operate systems at peak performance.
