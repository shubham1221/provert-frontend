WHY CUSTOM CHAMBERS ?

Not Every Product Fits a Standard
Chamber.

Standard environmental test chambers are designed for common testing applications. However, some products require chamber dimensions, temperature ranges, humidity conditions, airflow patterns or control systems that cannot be achieved with an off-the-shelf configuration.

Automotive components, EV batteries, electronic assemblies, aerospace parts, defence equipment and large industrial products may require specialized chamber configurations based on product size, test method and applicable standards.

Provert engineers each custom chamber around the product and test requirement. Chamber dimensions, temperature range, cooling capacity, heating system, humidity control, sensors, data logging, PLC controls and safety systems can be configured according to the required testing process.

FREQUENTLY ASK QUESTIONS

Questions about custom environmental chambers.

A custom environmental test chamber is a testing system engineered around specific product dimensions, temperature and humidity conditions, test volume, control requirements and testing procedures. Provert designs and manufactures custom chambers when a standard environmental chamber cannot meet the required application or testing specifications.

A custom chamber may be required when the product is oversized, has unusual dimensions, requires a specific temperature or humidity range, needs specialized fixtures or requires integration with external testing and automation systems. Provert evaluates the product, test method and operating requirements before developing the appropriate chamber configuration.

Provert can customize chamber dimensions, temperature range, humidity control, internal fixtures, access arrangements, control systems, sensors, data logging, safety features and automation interfaces according to the application. The available configuration depends on the required test conditions, product characteristics and chamber size.

Yes. Provert can design custom environmental test chambers for large or oversized products where standard chamber dimensions are not suitable. Chamber size, door configuration, internal working space, airflow and environmental control systems can be engineered around the product dimensions and required testing conditions.

Yes. Provert can engineer custom environmental test chambers around applicable testing standards and test procedures. The chamber configuration can be developed according to the required temperature, humidity, cycling, exposure time, test volume and measurement conditions specified by the relevant application or standard.

Yes. PLC-based controls, HMI interfaces, sensors and data logging can be incorporated into custom environmental test chambers based on the application requirements. Control and monitoring configurations can be designed to provide consistent operation and record relevant test parameters during testing.

Yes. Where technically applicable, Provert can configure custom chambers for integration with existing automation or laboratory systems. Control interfaces, communication requirements, sensors and other integration points are considered during the engineering and design stage to ensure compatibility with the intended testing process.

Provert follows a structured process covering requirement analysis, engineering design, 3D CAD development, customer approval, manufacturing, factory testing, installation and commissioning. Each stage is used to verify the chamber configuration and ensure that the final equipment meets the agreed application and performance requirements.

Yes. Provert can evaluate existing environmental test chambers for application-specific modifications or upgrades. Depending on the equipment and requirement, modifications may include chamber configuration, fixtures, sensors, controls, access arrangements and selected mechanical or electrical components.

Yes. Provert provides installation and commissioning support for custom environmental test chambers. The process can include equipment installation, utility and electrical checks, system inspection, operational testing and commissioning to help ensure that the chamber is ready for its intended testing application.
