OUR SERVICES

Technical support throughout your
equipment lifecycle.

01

Installation & Commissioning

Our service engineers provide on-site installation and commissioning for environmental testing equipment. Services include equipment positioning, utility connections, refrigeration or cooling-system integration where applicable, electrical verification, calibration checks and system run-in before handover.

02

Fabrication & Customization

Provert provides equipment modification and fabrication support for application-specific testing requirements. Chamber dimensions, fixtures, access arrangements, controls and selected mechanical or electrical components can be configured according to product and testing requirements.

03

Repair & Overhaul

Our service engineers diagnose equipment faults and restore testing systems with minimal downtime. Support can include refrigeration troubleshooting, compressor-related issues, sensor replacement, electrical repairs, controller issues and component replacement, depending on equipment configuration.

04

AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts)

Provert's AMC services provide scheduled preventive maintenance, equipment health checks and priority breakdown support. Maintenance activities are planned around equipment usage and operating conditions to reduce unexpected downtime and help maintain consistent equipment performance.

05

Consultation & Engineering Audits

Our technical team can support equipment selection, testing requirements, chamber suitability and environmental testing applications. We help customers evaluate existing equipment and identify appropriate technical configurations based on product requirements and applicable test methods.

06

Calibration & Validation

Provert provides calibration and performance verification services for environmental testing equipment, including temperature, humidity and other relevant parameters. Calibration and validation activities can be documented through appropriate calibration records, test reports and measurement data based on the equipment and application requirements.

07

Technical Support & Operator Training

Provert provides technical assistance, troubleshooting guidance and operator training to help customers use and maintain environmental testing equipment effectively. Training can cover equipment operation, controller settings, routine maintenance, safety procedures and basic troubleshooting.

01 What services does Provert provide for environmental testing equipment?

Provert provides technical services for environmental testing equipment, including installation and commissioning, calibration and validation, preventive maintenance, AMC, repair and troubleshooting, equipment modification, operator training and technical support. Services are planned according to the equipment configuration, testing application and operating requirements.

02 Do you provide installation and commissioning for environmental test chambers?

Yes. Provert provides installation and commissioning services for environmental test chambers, including equipment positioning, utility and electrical checks, system inspection and operational testing. Our team ensures the equipment is properly set up and ready for the intended testing application.

03 Do you offer AMC for environmental test chambers?

Yes. Provert provides Annual Maintenance Contracts for environmental test chambers, including scheduled preventive maintenance, equipment health checks and breakdown support. Maintenance activities are planned according to equipment type, usage and operating conditions to help reduce unexpected downtime and maintain reliable testing performance.

04 Do you provide calibration and validation services for test chambers?

Yes. Provert provides calibration and performance verification services for environmental testing equipment, including relevant temperature and humidity measurements. Calibration and validation activities can include measurement checks, test reports and calibration records based on the equipment and application requirements.

05 Can Provert repair and service an existing environmental test chamber?

Yes. Provert provides repair and servicing support for existing environmental test chambers, depending on the equipment configuration and issue.
