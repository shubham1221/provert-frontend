The Corrosion Test Chamber is engineered to replicate harsh environmental conditions for evaluating corrosion resistance of materials, coatings, and components. It supports salt spray testing, CASS testing, and cyclic corrosion testing, making it a versatile solution for industrial applications.


The system provides precise control over temperature, humidity, and fog generation, ensuring stable and repeatable testing conditions. This allows manufacturers to assess product durability under accelerated corrosion environments and improve product quality and compliance.

FAQS
Frequently asked questions
1. What types of corrosion tests can be performed in this chamber?

The chamber supports salt spray testing, CASS testing, and cyclic corrosion testing, enabling evaluation of materials and coatings under various environmental conditions.

2. What is the advantage of cyclic corrosion testing?

Cyclic corrosion testing simulates real-world environmental variations by alternating between humidity, temperature, and salt exposure, providing more realistic results.

3. How does the chamber ensure consistent corrosion conditions?

It uses controlled fog spray, temperature regulation, and humidity control along with uniform fog distribution to maintain stable testing conditions.

4. Can the system be monitored remotely?

Yes, it supports wireless and LAN connectivity for real-time monitoring and control via mobile devices and computers.

5. Is the chamber suitable for continuous industrial testing?

Yes, it is designed for long-duration testing with stable performance and built-in safety systems.

6. Can the chamber be customized?

Yes, it can be customized in terms of size, materials, control systems, and testing cycles to meet specific requirements.
